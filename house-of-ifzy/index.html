How ordering works

From your message to your door.

  1. Step one

    Send us the piece

    Tap order on anything here and WhatsApp opens with the item already written in. Tell us the size, shade or length you want.

  2. Before dispatch

    Pay by transfer

    We confirm the piece is available and send you a total with delivery. Payment is by bank transfer, before the item goes out.

  3. Before it ships

    See it first

    We show you your exact item online before it leaves us, so you know what is coming. Please look carefully, as we do not take returns.

  4. 5 days or less

    It reaches you

    Within Lagos, expect it in five days or less. Other states allow up to seven days processing. Delivery cost depends on where you are.
